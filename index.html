About Dice

Clothing with a point of view

What we do. Dice designs and sells everyday clothing and accessories for people who care about fit, fabric and finish.

Our story. Dice began in 2021 as a small print project. It grew into a full label by focusing on one idea: fewer pieces, made better. (Sample history for this project.)
