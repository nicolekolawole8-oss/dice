About Dice

Fashion that takes a chance on you.

Dice designs and sells premium clothing and fashion accessories: hoodies, dresses, footwear, bags and more. Every piece is designed in-house, produced in small batches with ethical partners, and finished with the signature details our customers love.

Beyond products, we offer personal styling, custom tailoring and fast delivery, so every customer leaves looking and feeling like their best self.

Our story so far

  1. 2018

    Two friends roll the dice, launching Dice from a tiny studio with just 12 hoodie designs.

  2. 2020

    Our first flagship store opens, and the black and pink identity becomes our signature.

  3. 2023

    We go global online and expand into footwear and luxury accessories.

  4. 2026

    More than 50,000 customers in 18 cities, and we're still just getting started.
